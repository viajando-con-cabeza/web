Before exploring Fuerteventura

PLAN YOUR TRIP

Practical information based on our trip in June 2025.

Trip base

We stayed for three nights in Caleta de Fuste, a practical location for heading out to a different area each day.

Rental car

Pick-up and drop-off at the airport. For this route, it is the most convenient way to connect the north, south, and the interior.

Route Pace

Days 2 and 3 are the most intense. It is advisable to set off early and allow plenty of time for slow roads, stops, and viewpoints.
