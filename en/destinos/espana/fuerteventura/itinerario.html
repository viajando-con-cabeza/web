ITINERARY SUMMARY

Four days by car with loop routes through each area of the island.

4 days, four zones

The first day is easy due to the arrival. Afterwards, we tour the north, dedicate a full day to Jandía and Cofete, and finish through the interior and the west coast before the return flight.
