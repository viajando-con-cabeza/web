HOW TO SAVE

Money-saving ideas for accommodation, transport, and activities in Sweden.

Book accommodation in advance

Compare hotels, cabins, and apartments ahead of time for the most popular destinations.

Mix your transport

Use trains and buses for connected cities; rent a car if your rural route requires it.

Choose your dates wisely

Seasonality and location significantly impact prices, especially in summer and winter.

Free nature

Lakes, forests, viewpoints, and walks allow you to fill your days without buying tickets.

Check the ferries

Consult schedules and prices before planning your archipelago days.

Prioritize experiences

Budget for your favorite activities and avoid paying for passes you won't use.
