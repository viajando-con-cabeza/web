First, decide how to travel

HOW TO TRAVEL AROUND SWEDEN

To connect cities, we prioritize the train; for villages, the bus; and for islands, ferries. If you are exploring lakes or less connected nature, consider renting a car.

To get between cities

TRAIN

Between connected cities
Pros and cons
  • ✓Fast between major cities
  • ✓Stations near the city center
  • ✓Avoid driving and parking
  • ✕Less flexible for rural areas
How to pay

Ticket price depends on distance, date, and operator; check if changes are allowed.

When to use it

Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö, and other intercity connections.

To save money while traveling

BUS

Budget-friendly towns and routes
Pros and cons
  • ✓Reaches towns without train access
  • ✓Can be more economical
  • ✓Complements train journeys
  • ✕Usually takes longer than the train
How to pay

Regional or intercity ticket, depending on the line and route.

When to use it

When the train doesn't reach or the bus fits your route better.

To reach the north

Long distances and northern Sweden

  • ✓Useful if you have only a few days
  • ✕Transfers, luggage, and waiting times
How to pay

Add the flight, luggage, and travel to and from the airports.

If you combine Stockholm with Kiruna, Luleå, or Umeå, for example.

TRAIN

National rail network · intercity connections

Infrastructure
  • The country has unos 15.600 km de vías, nearly 80% electrified.
  • Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö form the axis for high-speed and intercity trains.
  • Regional and commuter services connect towns and cities.
  • There are overnight trains heading north to cover long distances.
Keep in mind
  • SJ, VR, Vy, Snälltåget, and regional companies operate these routes; compare your options.
  • On popular routes, booking early usually works out better.
  • Check for construction, transfers, the station, and the last train before booking.
  • For isolated villages, check the connecting bus: the train does not reach everywhere.
VCC TIP

We would choose the train between cities; for rural routes, we would combine modes.

BUS

Regional and intercity network · towns and cities

Infrastructure
  • Complete your train journey and reach villages and areas without a station.
  • Networks such as SL (Stockholm), Västtrafik (west) and Skånetrafiken (south).
  • Each region has its own operators, fare zones, and tickets.
  • For long distances, there are services like FlixBus and Vy bus4you.
Keep in mind
  • Check the regional website or app: a pass from one region won't work throughout all of Sweden.
  • Outside of cities, there are fewer services at night and on holidays.
  • Check the last bus before choosing an excursion or accommodation.
  • Allow time for transfers and double-check the exact departure stop.
VCC TIP

We would combine it with the train; we would avoid routes with too many changes.

FLIGHT

Domestic airports · long distances and the north

Infrastructure
  • Swedavia manages a network of 10 airports in the country.
  • Arlanda is the major airport for Stockholm; Landvetter serves Gothenburg.
  • Kiruna, Luleå, Umeå, and Åre/Östersund help connect the north.
  • Visby provides air connections to Gotland; Malmö and Ronneby cover areas in the south.
Keep in mind
  • To travel far north, you can save hours compared to traveling by land.
  • Calculate transfers, security, and waiting times, not just flight hours.
  • Check frequencies, layovers, and what the baggage allowance includes.
  • Compare the total cost with daytime and overnight trains.
VCC TIP

We would only fly if the total time saved makes it worthwhile.

CAR

Main roads · scenic routes

Infrastructure
  • The E4 runs through much of the country from south to north.
  • The E6 connects the west coast and passes through Gothenburg.
  • Secondary roads provide access to forests, lakes, and villages.
  • It is useful for linking various accommodations or rural stops in a single day.
Keep in mind
  • Add rental, fuel, insurance, and parking to the price.
  • In Stockholm and Gothenburg, there may be congestion charges.
  • Winter conditions require specific tire requirements.
  • Check road conditions and restrictions before a long route.
VCC TIP

We would rent a car for nature, not for visiting cities.

FERRY

Sea routes · islands and archipelagos

Infrastructure
  • Ferries are a common mode of transport in Swedish archipelagos and islands.
  • Waxholmsbolaget connects numerous islands around Stockholm.
  • Destination Gotland links Visby with Nynäshamn and Oskarshamn.
  • There are urban and regional ferries, as well as vehicle-friendly routes.
Keep in mind
  • Schedules change seasonally: check the last ferry.
  • Check your departure pier and how to get there.
  • Some routes require reservations; check the rules for cars or bicycles.
  • Allow extra time when connecting with trains and keep the schedule in mind.
VCC TIP

When visiting islands, we always check the schedules and the last ferry.

What makes sense for your trip

RENT A CAR?

In cities, public transport is better; on rural routes, compare costs.

WHEN WE WOULD RENT ONE

Ideal for road trips and traveling with freedom.

  • You are going to visit villages, lakes, or nature.
  • You want to make multiple stops in a single day.
  • Your route has few connections via public transport.
  • There are several of you and you can split the costs.

WHEN WE WOULD NOT RENT ONE

For cities and well-connected routes, it is usually not worth it.

  • You visit Stockholm, Gothenburg, or Malmö.
  • You travel between well-connected locations by train or bus.
  • All you need is punctual journeys.
  • You prefer to avoid parking, tolls, and driving in the city.

HOW TO SAVE

Seven tips for spending less on your travels through Sweden.

Compare operators

Not all trains appear at the same price or with the same conditions. Check more than one option before paying.

Book with flexibility

On long-distance trains, changing the day or time can significantly affect the price. Check if the ticket is modifiable.

Don't confuse the networks

SL, Västtrafik, and Skånetrafiken are different regional networks. A city pass does not automatically cover other regions.

Consider buses

If the train is expensive or doesn't reach your destination, check the intercity lines and their exact stations.

Calculate the full cost of a car

Rental prices exclude fuel, parking, potential tolls, and one-way drop-off fees.

Prepare for long journeys

For the north, compare overnight trains, flights, and final transfers. Check for railway works and connections before booking.

Allow for buffer time when transferring

If you purchase tickets from different companies, avoid tight connections and review the terms of each booking.
