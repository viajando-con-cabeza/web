OUR OPINION

It is the area with the most character for a first visit. We would choose Gamla Stan if we wanted to walk out into the historic streets and didn't mind paying more for that location. We would recommend it to those who value historical atmosphere over room size or price. It is worth checking if the street is noisy and if the building has an elevator.

THINGS TO KEEP IN MIND

  • It can be noisy and touristy.

HOW TO SAVE

Tips for sleeping well and paying less in Stockholm.

BOOK IN ADVANCE

Compare prices ahead of time, especially in summer and during high-demand dates.

COMPARE NEIGHBORHOODS

Solna, Kungsholmen, or Södermalm may offer better value for money than the city center.

CHECK THE METRO

A nearby metro stop can make up for staying a bit further away and paying less.

FLEXIBLE DATES

Check different check-in and check-out dates: rates can vary significantly.

FLEXIBLE CANCELLATION

If the price works for you, consider a refundable rate so you can compare options later.

CHECK THE FINAL PRICE

Check for taxes, breakfast, cleaning fees, and any other surcharges before deciding.

CHOOSE WHAT YOU NEED

Avoid paying for extras you won't use; having a kitchen can help you reduce food expenses.
