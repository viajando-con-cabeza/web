Prioritize what makes the difference

WHERE TO SPEND AND SAVE

Based on our experience, what's worth the extra budget and what isn't.

Invest in memories

Budget for what makes your trip special.

✓
Experiences you will remember

If you are excited to explore the archipelago, set aside part of your budget for a boat trip. Prioritize one experience you will truly remember over several activities that don't interest you as much.

✓
Culture your way

Choose the museums that truly match your tastes; the Vasa, for example, might be worth it if you are interested in history. You don't need to go into all of them: take your time and enjoy the ones you select.

✓
Savor the journey

Save some budget for a special meal that you are truly craving. For the rest of the days, you can alternate between cafes, food markets, and simple options without giving up on enjoying yourself.

✓
Rest well

Pay for a good night's sleep, for cleanliness, and for easy access to public transport. A good location doesn't have to be the most central or the most expensive.

✓
Time to enjoy

Leave some breathing room in your itinerary for that one plan you are most looking forward to, even if it means investing a little more. Avoiding the rush can also greatly improve the experience.

✓
Small moments

Set aside some time to sit in a cafe and enjoy a fika without looking at the clock. Sometimes the most beautiful memories aren't from the most expensive plans.

Save money without missing out

Pay for what you enjoy, and find alternatives for everything else.

×
Travel smart

Before paying for a taxi, check if you can get there by metro, bus, or tram. Compare the prices of single tickets and passes based on your planned routes.

×
A more local fika

Don't just stick to the cafes on the busiest streets. Explore the neighborhoods you pass through and compare options before deciding where to take a break.

×
Stay well-connected

Accommodation near a metro station can offer better value for money than something right in the city center. Also, check the travel time, not just the distance on the map.

×
Intentional souvenirs

Choose one or two souvenirs that you truly love instead of impulse buying at every stop. This helps you avoid small expenses that end up adding up significantly.

×
Plan your meals

Look up some dining options before arriving in a particularly touristy area. Having two or three alternatives saved will help you choose without rushing or making unplanned expenses.

×
Set your priorities

Don't buy tickets just to check places off a list. Select the sights you are most excited about and leave room to wander and discover the city without paying for every single activity.

Small changes, big difference

HOW TO SAVE

Real tips for enjoying Stockholm on a budget.

Public transport

Compare passes and tickets based on your actual routes. A taxi would rarely be our first choice.

Stay connected

Prioritize metro access and connectivity over being located exactly in Gamla Stan or Norrmalm.

Mix up your meals

Combine food halls, cafes, and simple options with one or two special meals.

Free activities

Neighborhoods, parks, viewpoints, and waterfront walks can fill hours without an entrance fee.

Choose your dates wisely

Compare seasons and daylight hours: accommodation prices can vary significantly.

Use the water

When it makes sense, the ferry can be both transportation and an experience at the same time.

Watch out for this

MISTAKES TO AVOID

Avoid decisions that drive up your travel costs without adding real value.

Taxis and private transport

Stockholm has great public transport; a taxi can make even a short trip very expensive.

High season

Accommodation prices can rise significantly during high demand periods and the summer.

Museum entrance fees

Multiple tickets in a single day add up quickly: it's better to choose the ones that truly interest you. Check our Stockholm tickets and passes guide to see what to book.

Eating in tourist areas

In Gamla Stan and highly visited areas, you might pay more without the experience being any better.
