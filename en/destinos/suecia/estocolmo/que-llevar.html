What we learned while exploring Stockholm

OUR VCC EXPERIENCE

Our actual itinerary · 3 days

We explored Gamla Stan and the city center, visited Djurgården, and headed out to Vaxholm and Grinda. The key to packing isn't bringing many different outfits: it's being able to walk comfortably and having an outer layer handy for boat trips.

View our 3-day itinerary

✓ For walking around the city

  • Comfortable footwear with good soles for walking days
  • Layered clothing, easy to adapt when entering museums

↻ For the ferry and the islands

  • Windbreaker or lightweight raincoat depending on the month
  • Small backpack to keep an extra layer handy

× What we wouldn't add

  • A completely different outfit for every day
  • A second pair of uncomfortable or bulky shoes
