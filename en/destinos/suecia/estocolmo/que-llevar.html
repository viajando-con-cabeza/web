Our way of packing

OUR VCC EXPERIENCE

In Stockholm, we prioritize versatile layers, comfortable footwear, and protection against wind and rain. Here is what we would pack again and what we would avoid when preparing our suitcase.

✓ What we would prioritize

  • Versatile pieces that work in layers
  • Comfortable footwear for walking all day
  • A jacket for rain and wind

× What we would avoid

  • Too many different outfits for each day
  • Uncomfortable shoes that take up space
  • Bulky coats in summer

↻ If we went back...

  • We would choose more versatile and easy-to-wash clothing
  • We would pack a compact raincoat
  • We would leave room for some shopping

Small changes, more space

HOW TO SAVE SPACE

Simple ideas to organize your clothes, make the most of every corner of your suitcase, and avoid carrying unnecessary weight. Swipe to see all the tips.

Organize by bags

Use packing cubes to separate clothes, accessories, and footwear.

Mix and match

Choose versatile clothing that allows you to create several outfits with fewer pieces.

Roll lightweight items

Rolling thin garments makes the most of small gaps in your suitcase.

See how bulky it is

Wear your bulkiest coat and footwear during the journey.

Leave some free space

Don't overpack your suitcase: leave room for souvenirs or shopping.

Choose mini formats

Carry toiletries and cosmetics in small, reusable containers.
