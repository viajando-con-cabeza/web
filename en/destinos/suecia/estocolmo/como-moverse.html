WALKING + METRO + FERRY

Walk through Gamla Stan and the alternative neighborhoods, use the metro only when it makes sense, and take advantage of your SL ticket for the regular ferry to Vaxholm.

    HOW TO SAVE

    Tips for getting around Stockholm without overspending.

    SL ticket or pass?

    With adult fares, the 24-hour pass pays for itself after 5 trips; the 72-hour pass after 9. If you plan on walking a lot, count your trips before buying.

    Start with your hotel

    Choose well-connected accommodation and group your visits by neighborhood. This will help you avoid unnecessary back-and-forth travel.

    Walk through the center

    Gamla Stan and much of Norrmalm are best enjoyed on foot. Don't pay for the metro just to cross a few streets.

    Public ferries

    Check which ferries are included in the SL network before paying for an excursion. Fares depend on the route.

    Museums in one day

    Group the Vasa Museum, Skansen, and other nearby museums together. Get to Djurgården by tram or ferry and continue on foot.

    If you are driving

    Check the parking costs before entering the city center. Parking on the outskirts and using SL may be cheaper.

    Bicycles without extras

    Compare helmets, deposits, insurance, and return policies. Only rent the bicycle hours you actually need.
