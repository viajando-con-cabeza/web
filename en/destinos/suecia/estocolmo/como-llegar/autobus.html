🚌 How to get to Stockholm by bus

Long-distance buses may drop you off near the city center or at different stops depending on the operator.

Available options

  • National lines from other Swedish cities.
  • Seasonal long-distance international connections.
  • Pedestrian access or via metro and urban transport from the arrival station.

VCC Tips

  • Cityterminalen is located next to the major central railway hub.
  • By bus, travel time and the arrival station depend heavily on the route.

How to get there step by step

  1. Identify the stop or arrival station specified by your carrier.
  2. Check whether you are disembarking at Cityterminalen or another point in the city.
  3. Check the local transit route to your accommodation and, if possible, buy your ticket in advance.
  4. Allow extra time if you have a train or ferry connection.
