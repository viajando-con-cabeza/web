🚆 How to get to Stockholm by train

Stockholm Central is the city's main railway hub; from there, you can continue on foot or connect with the urban transit network.

Available options

  • National and intercity trains from Swedish cities.
  • International connections subject to route, dates, and operators.
  • Metro, commuter trains, buses, and walking routes from the central area.

VCC Tips

  • The central station is already centrally located: for some hotels, you won't need any transport.
  • Avoid assuming that all train tickets include urban transit.

How to get there step by step

  1. Find your train and check your ticket for the exact arrival station.
  2. Upon arriving at Stockholm Central, follow the signs toward the exit or the urban connection you need.
  3. Check SL for the best route to your accommodation and verify if you need a separate ticket.
  4. If you are traveling with luggage, compare walking with taking the metro or a taxi for the final leg.
