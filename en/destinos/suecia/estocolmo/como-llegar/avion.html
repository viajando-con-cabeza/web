✈️ How to get to Stockholm by plane

Stockholm has several airports. Before booking, check which one your flight uses and how much the total journey to your accommodation costs.

Airports and connections

  • Arlanda (ARN): the main international gateway, with trains and buses to the city center.
  • Bromma (BMA): an airport close to the city; check which routes are operational before booking.
  • Skavsta (NYO): an airport much further away; calculate the road transfer cost before deciding.

VCC Tips

  • Arlanda Express usually saves time, but it isn't necessarily the most economical option.
  • Compare the total price of the flight and the transfer: a distant airport can end up being more expensive.
  • Verify rates and schedules on official websites before your trip.

Which airport is best for you?

01 · ARN

Arlanda

The main international airport. To get to the center, you can choose between high-speed train, commuter train, bus, or taxi.

To save money: compare the total price and check for potential rail supplements; the fastest option isn't always the cheapest.

Airport Information ↗

02 · BMA

Bromma

Closest to Stockholm, but with a limited and fluctuating flight selection. Check routes and available urban transport for your dates.

To decide: don't choose based on distance alone: check the flight schedule and actual transfer time.

Airport Information ↗

03 · NYO

Skavsta

It is located quite a bit further from the city. The bus and road connections are usually the most important part of the transfer.

To save money: add flight, transit, and time before choosing a 'low cost' fare.

How to compare transfers to the city center

High-speed trainThe priority is to arrive early; check the price.
Regional / commuter trainAlternative for some airports; check stops and surcharges.
BusCompare departure times, baggage allowance, and final stop.
Taxi / transferUseful for groups or at night, but confirm cost and conditions.

How to get there step by step

  1. Confirm the exact airport in your booking, not just the name "Stockholm".
  2. Compare express trains, regional or commuter trains, buses, and taxis depending on the airport.
  3. Check the terminal and where your chosen transport departs from; purchase tickets through the official operator.
  4. Arrive at Stockholm Central or your final stop and transfer to the metro, bus, or commuter train if needed.
