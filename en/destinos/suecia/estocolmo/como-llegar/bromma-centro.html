How to get from Bromma to Stockholm city center

How to check connections and organize the final leg of your journey from a nearby airport.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

Bromma is closer to the city than Arlanda, but flight availability and transfer options may vary. First, check that your flight actually operates out of BMA.

Urban transport options depend on the time of day and available connections. Use the SL planner with your accommodation's exact address.

A taxi or a pre-booked transfer can be useful if you arrive with luggage or during off-peak hours.

VCC Tip

Being closer doesn't necessarily mean it's more convenient: check the frequency and actual cost before deciding.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Confirm the BMA code in your reservation.
  2. Check at the airport for active stops and connections for that day.
  3. Calculate the route with SL from the departure stop to your accommodation.
  4. Compare the door-to-door cost with a taxi at a fixed rate.
