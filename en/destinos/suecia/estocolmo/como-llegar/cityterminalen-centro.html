How to get from Cityterminalen to Stockholm city center

A guide to leaving the bus station and continuing to your accommodation.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

Cityterminalen is located right next to the Stockholm Central area, a very convenient location for reaching the city center.

Depending on your accommodation, you can walk or connect with the metro, commuter trains, and SL city buses.

Not all long-distance coaches stop at Cityterminalen: check your stop in the company confirmation.

VCC Tip

If the coach arrives at Cityterminalen and you are staying downtown, check the walking distance beforehand.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Check the exact stop on your bus ticket.
  2. Locate the exit that brings you closer to your hotel or the transport hub.
  3. If you cannot walk, look up a route with SL.
  4. Allow for extra time if you are connecting to other transport.
