🚗 How to get to Stockholm by car

A car can be practical for exploring Sweden or visiting rural areas, but parking in the city center can significantly increase the cost of your trip.

Where to park and how to save money

  • City center parking: convenient, but check rates, restrictions, and hours.
  • Park outside the city center and continue by metro, train, or bus.
  • Reservable spaces: compare apps and cancellation policies before paying.

VCC Tips

  • For an urban stay, compare the cost of parking your car all day versus using public transport.
  • A "free" parking spot may be subject to specific hours, permits, or time limits.
  • We do not recommend specific spaces without verifying current regulations and rates.

Where is the best place to park?

OPTION 01

Park near your accommodation

The easiest alternative if the hotel offers a space. Ask about the 24-hour rate, access, maximum height, and entry restrictions.

Ideal for: short stays or trips with lots of luggage.

OPTION 02

Park on the outskirts

Look for an authorized parking lot near a metro or train station and also factor in the cost of city transit tickets.

Ideal for: those leaving their car parked for several days and looking to reduce expenses.

OPTION 03

Book a spot

Compare parking options on operator websites or apps. Check the cancellation policy, entry times, and whether the spot is guaranteed.

Ideal for: peak hours or when you need certainty.

Car rental in Stockholm

If you need a car to travel around Sweden, compare the actual booking conditions before choosing a company. Among the companies you can consult are Hertz, Avis, Sixt y Europcar; availability depends on the specific branch and date.

  • Pick-up: compare airports, stations, and city offices; prices are not always the same.
  • Actual price: check the deductible, coverage, additional driver fees, fuel policy, and mileage limits.
  • Drop-off: check operating hours and potential one-way fees for returning to a different city.

We do not recommend a specific company without comparing the offers available for your trip.

How to get there step by step

  1. Before entering the city, locate your accommodation and check if it has parking.
  2. Review local traffic regulations, potential congestion charges, and parking zones.
  3. Compare parking near your hotel with parking on the outskirts and using public transport.
  4. Check the payment terms, operating hours, maximum vehicle height, and whether it is a public parking lot.
