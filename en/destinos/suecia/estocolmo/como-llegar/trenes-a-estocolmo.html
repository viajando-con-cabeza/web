How to get to Stockholm by train from other cities

Tickets, stations, and transfers for traveling from Sweden and nearby countries.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

The train is an interesting alternative for connecting Stockholm with other Swedish cities. Routes and frequencies depend on your origin and the operator.

National trains can arrive at Stockholm Central and other stations. Check the exact arrival point on your ticket.

For international connections, check the entire route and the buffer between trains; do not assume it is a single direct service.

VCC Tip

The train is especially practical when you are already in Sweden and want to avoid a second flight.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Look for the full route between your city of origin and Stockholm.
  2. Compare schedules, transfers, and baggage policies.
  3. Confirm the final station and the transfer buffer.
  4. Plan your final leg with SL if your accommodation is not nearby.
