How to get from Stockholm Central to the city center

Leaving the station and getting to your hotel by walking, metro, or bus.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

Stockholm Central is located in a very central area. If your accommodation is nearby, walking might be the most convenient and economical option.

For further areas, check the metro (Tunnelbana), commuter trains, and SL buses. Connections and tickets will depend on your exact destination.

The station has several exits and connections. Before heading out, locate your spot on the map to avoid wandering around with luggage.

VCC Tip

If you are staying near the station, avoid paying for a transfer: many parts of the city center are within walking distance.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Check your ticket to see if you are arriving at Stockholm Central or another station.
  2. Check your hotel's address and see if you can walk there.
  3. If you need public transport, plan your route using SL.
  4. Follow the exit or transfer signs and keep your required ticket.
