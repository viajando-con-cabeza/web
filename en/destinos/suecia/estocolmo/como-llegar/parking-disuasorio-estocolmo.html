Parking outside the center and using public transport

How to combine peripheral parking with the metro or train to save money.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

Leaving your car outside the center and entering via public transport can be a good strategy if you are spending several days in Stockholm.

Not all peripheral parking lots allow long stays or have the same rules: check the parking space before you travel.

When comparing, add up the parking fee, SL tickets for all travelers, and the travel time to your accommodation.

VCC Tip

If you are not going to use the car during your visit, our first comparison would be outer parking plus SL.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Choose a metro or train station that has good connections to your accommodation.
  2. Look for legal parking nearby and check their terms and conditions.
  3. Check the round-trip public transport price for everyone.
  4. Make sure the parking lot allows you to leave your car for the intended duration.
