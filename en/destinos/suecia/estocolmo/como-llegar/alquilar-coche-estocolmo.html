Car rental in Stockholm: companies and tips

Offices, deductibles, insurance, and terms you should compare.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

To visit only Stockholm, you usually don't need a car. If you plan a route through Sweden or visit rural areas, it can offer more flexibility.

Compare airport and city offices, opening hours, mileage limits, fuel policies, and one-way drop-off fees.

Check the deductible, excluded damages, and how roadside assistance works before choosing an offer.

VCC Tip

If you arrive in Stockholm for a road trip, we would pick up the car when leaving the city, whenever feasible.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Decide if you really need a car during your city days.
  2. Compare different offices and companies, not just the daily price.
  3. Review insurance, deductibles, required cards, and deposits.
  4. Confirm pick-up, drop-off, fuel, and winter conditions.
