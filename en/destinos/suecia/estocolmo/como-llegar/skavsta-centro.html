How to get from Skavsta to Stockholm city center

The remote airport: transfers, travel times, and how to avoid surprises.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

Skavsta is quite far from Stockholm city center. Although a flight might seem cheap, it is worth factoring in transport, luggage, and time.

Airport coaches are one of the regular connections to the center; check if the schedules align with your flight arrival.

If you have to make several transfers, compare the total cost and the arrival time at your accommodation. For night arrivals, check the services very carefully.

VCC Tip

We wouldn't choose Skavsta just for a cheap ticket: first, we would add up the transfer costs and travel time.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Confirm that you are arriving at Skavsta (NYO), not Arlanda.
  2. Look for available buses that match your flight schedule.
  3. Check the bus arrival station and subsequent local transport options.
  4. Add the transfer price to the price of your plane ticket.
