⛴️ How to get to Stockholm by ferry

Sea arrivals may disembark at different terminals in the Stockholm area, depending on the route and the company.

Available options

  • Ferry and cruise terminals in the Stockholm area.
  • Connections from the Baltic Sea, subject to the shipping company and schedule.
  • Metro, bus, commuter train, or taxi for the final leg.

VCC Tips

  • Do not confuse the departure port, the cruise pier, and the ferry terminal.
  • Check your shipping line's instructions: terminals do not always share the same connections.

How to get there step by step

  1. Check your ticket for the terminal name and its exact address.
  2. Find the nearest public transport stop and check the schedule on SL.
  3. Factor in the time between disembarking and accessing transport, especially when carrying luggage.
  4. Consider a taxi or private transfer if the terminal is far away or if you are arriving at night.
