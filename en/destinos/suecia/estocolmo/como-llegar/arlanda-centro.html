How to get from Arlanda to Stockholm city center

Express train, commuter train, bus, and taxi: how to choose without overpaying.

← All ways to get there

What you need to know

The Arlanda Express connects the airport to Stockholm Central in about 18 minutes. It is convenient if speed is your priority, but check the price for your specific date.

The SL commuter train uses Arlanda Central in SkyCity. The train journey takes around 38 minutes; check for the station access surcharge in addition to the ticket price.

Flygbussarna buses connect the airport to Cityterminalen. They can be competitively priced, though they are subject to traffic.

A taxi or private transfer can be worth it if you are traveling in a group and have a lot of luggage. Confirm a fixed price before getting in.

VCC Tip

If you're short on time, compare the Arlanda Express; if you want to save money, calculate the cost of the bus and the commuter train with the surcharge.

Always check current schedules and terms before your trip.

How to organize your journey

  1. Identify your terminal and follow the public transport signs.
  2. Compare the total price, including any regional train surcharges.
  3. Purchase your ticket on the operator's official website or app.
  4. From Stockholm Central or Cityterminalen, continue to your accommodation via SL or on foot.
