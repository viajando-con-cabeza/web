Stockholm in 3 days

Ancient city, museums and archipelago on our royal route.

Travel VCC: from 24 to 26 August 2026.We review each year prices, schedules, transportation, tickets and availability.

A first day to tour the old town, a second day to discover museums and corners of Stockholm, and a third to visit Vaxholm and Grinda in the archipelago and finish the day in Sigtuna.

The schedules are indicative: enjoy each stop without turning the trip into a race.To prepare the day in the archipelago, consult our Transportation comparison between Stockholm and Vaxholm and check the schedules of the ships before leaving. Day 1: Where Magic Begins

Gamla Stan and the Center

Royal Palace, historic streets and walks by the water.

Day 1 · 25 August 2026

Start at Gamla Stan, the historic heart of the city, between colorful squares, paved streets and centuries-old buildings.In the morning you will discover the surroundings of the Royal Palace and the cathedral; then, a walk along the water will take you to Riddarholmen and the Town Hall.

Route hour by hour

See Map↗
  1. 09:00The Royal PalaceStart with the palace and its historic surroundings; consult the schedules if you want to visit the interior.
  2. 10:30Stortorget and its façadesStop at the square of the colourful façades and discover the streets that come out of it.
  3. 11:30Cathedral and historic streetsContinue walking between the cathedral and the passages of the old town, without following a rigid route.
  4. 13:00Food in Old TownTake a break to eat at Gamla Stan and rebuild forces before crossing to another island.
  5. 15:00Riddarholmen and its viewsGet close to the shore of Riddarholmen to contemplate the profile of the city from the water.
  6. 17:00City Hall of StockholmEnds next to the Town Hall and its gardens; first check out the interior visit options.
The VCC CouncilWe chose to start by Gamla Stan to walk through the historic center and leave Riddarholmen and the Town Hall for the afternoon.
Day 2: Nature and life by the sea

Vasa and Djurgården Museum

Museums, nature and a ferry ride through Stockholm.

Day 2 · 26 August 2026

Today it’s time to combine one of Stockholm’s most unique visits with the nature of Djurgården. The Vasa Museum allows you to get to know a 17th-century boat; when you leave, change the rhythm and enjoy the island’s green paths. Choose another cultural tour only if you like and end with the ferry journey: the views from the water are part of the experience.

Route hour by hour

See Map↗
  1. 09:30The Vasa MuseumDedicate the morning to know the ship of the 17th century and the history of its recovery.
  2. 12:00Walk through DjurgårdenWalk along the trails and green spaces of the island, leaving time to enjoy its corners.
  3. 13:30Food on the islandFind a quiet place to eat and rest before your next visit.
  4. 15:00Skansen or another museumChoose Skansen or another museum according to your interests and schedule; there is no need to cover it all.
  5. 18:00The return ferryReturn enjoying the views from the water and first check the frequency of the ferry.
The VCC CouncilWe would prioritize the Vasa Museum and leave time to walk around Djurgården. We prefer to enjoy the visits well before trying to enter all the museums.
Day 3: between islands and landscapes

Vaxholm, Grinda and Sigtuna

A tour of the islands and the historic village of Sigtuna.

Day 3 · 27 August 2026

This was our real third-day tour: first Vaxholm, then the island of Grinda and finally Sigtuna. We combined the landscape of the archipelago and boat trips with a walk through the historic streets of Sigtuna. Check out the connections available between each stage: boats and trips to Sigtuna require planning.

Route by Stage

See Map↗
  1. 01Departure from StockholmCheck the bridge and boat schedules for Vaxholm.
  2. 02VaxholmFirst stop of our route through the archipelago: walk around the village and enjoy its maritime atmosphere.
  3. 03Boat to GrindaNavigate between the islands and enjoy the views during the journey.
  4. 04GrindaWalk the trails, the forested areas and the island’s coast without rush.
  5. 05Transfer to SigtunaOrganize the return from Grinda and the land trip to Sigtuna according to the available hours.
  6. 06SigtunaEnd the day by strolling through the streets and traditional houses of this historic village.
