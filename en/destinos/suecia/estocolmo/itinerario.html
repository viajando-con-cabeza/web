VIEW BREAKDOWN
Day 1 · Where the magic begins

GAMLA STAN AND THE CITY CENTER

Royal Palace, historic alleys, and waterfront strolls.

DAY 1 · 25 AUG 2026

Start in Gamla Stan, the city's historic heart, among colorful squares, cobblestone alleys, and buildings that tell centuries of history. In the morning, you will discover the area around the Royal Palace and the cathedral; afterwards, a walk along the waterfront will take you to Riddarholmen and City Hall. The most special part is letting yourself be surprised by the passages and the views between the islands.

HOUR-BY-HOUR ROUTE

VIEW MAP↗
  1. 09:00Royal PalaceBegin with the palace and its historic surroundings; check opening times if you wish to visit the interior.
  2. 10:30Stortorget and its facadesStop at the square with the colorful facades and discover the alleys branching off from it.
  3. 11:30Cathedral and historic streetsContinue on foot between the cathedral and the old town passages, without following a rigid route.
  4. 13:00Dining in Gamla StanTake a break for lunch in Gamla Stan to recharge before crossing over to another island.
  5. 15:00Riddarholmen and its viewsHead to the edge of Riddarholmen to take in the city skyline from the water.
  6. 17:00Stockholm City HallEnd your journey by the City Hall and its gardens; check interior tour options in advance.
VCC TIPWe chose to start with Gamla Stan to explore the historic center on foot, leaving Riddarholmen and the City Hall for the afternoon. This way, we avoid unnecessary travel.
Day 2 · Nature and seaside life

VASA MUSEUM AND DJURGÅRDEN

Museums, nature, and a ferry ride through Stockholm.

DAY 2 · 26 AUG 2026

Today is about combining one of Stockholm's most unique visits with the nature of Djurgården. The Vasa Museum allows you to get up close to a 17th-century ship; once you step outside, change pace and enjoy the island's green paths. Only choose another cultural visit if you feel like it, and finish with a ferry ride: the views from the water are part of the experience.

HOUR-BY-HOUR ROUTE

VIEW MAP↗
  1. 09:30Vasa MuseumSpend the morning learning about the 17th-century ship and the history of its recovery.
  2. 12:00A stroll through DjurgårdenWalk along the island's paths and green spaces, leaving plenty of time to enjoy its hidden corners.
  3. 13:30Lunch on the islandFind a quiet place to eat and rest before your next visit.
  4. 15:00Skansen or another museumChoose Skansen or another museum based on your interests and opening hours; there is no need to see everything.
  5. 18:00Return ferryEnjoy the views from the water on your way back; be sure to check the ferry schedule in advance.
VCC TIPWe would prioritize the Vasa Museum and leave time to stroll through Djurgården. We prefer to truly enjoy our visits rather than trying to squeeze into every museum.
Day 3 · Between islands and landscapes

VAXHOLM, GRINDA, AND SIGTUNA

A tour of the islands and the historic town of Sigtuna.

DAY 3 · 27 AUG 2026

This was our actual itinerary for the third day: first Vaxholm, then the island of Grinda, and finally Sigtuna. We combined the archipelago scenery and boat trips with a walk through the historic streets of Sigtuna. Check the available connections between each stage: the boats and the travel to Sigtuna require planning.

STAGE-BY-STAGE ROUTE
