Nature in the city

PARKS AND WALKS

Discover Djurgården and the green corners by the water.

Djurgården

The perfect combination of museums, parks, and strolls. Even if you are visiting the Vasa, make sure to set aside time to step outside the building and walk.

Waterfront walks

You don't need to turn nature into a separate excursion. Part of the charm is how the water and green spaces appear right within the city.
