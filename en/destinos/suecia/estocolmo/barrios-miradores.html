STOCKHOLM NEIGHBORHOODS AND VIEWPOINTS

The part of the city that ensures your trip is about more than just monuments: atmosphere, cafes, walks, and great views.

OUR PRIORITY

Södermalm + Monteliusvägen

If you only have time to add one more neighborhood beyond the historic center, we think Södermalm is the most complete option for its atmosphere and views.

Södermalm

Cafés, streets with character, and a more local feel.

Monteliusvägen

A short walk with rewarding views toward Gamla Stan and the water.

Norrmalm

More functional and commercial, but useful for understanding contemporary Stockholm.

Östermalm

Elegant, orderly, and more residential.

Riddarholmen

A very photogenic stop within the historic setting.

By the water

There is no need to look for a formal viewpoint: many walks already offer stunning views.

FOR PHOTOS

When to go

The light changes the city significantly. If photography is important to you, schedule a visit to one of the viewpoints for late afternoon instead of fitting it in at any random time.
