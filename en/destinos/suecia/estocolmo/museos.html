WHICH MUSEUMS TO VISIT IN STOCKHOLM

I wouldn't try to visit them all. The key is to pick one or two that truly resonate with you.

QUICK PICK

If you only choose one: Vasa

For a first visit, the Vasa Museum is our top priority due to its uniqueness. From there, your second museum should depend much more on your personal interests.
