ISLANDS, FERRIES, AND WATERFRONT WALKS

In Stockholm, the water isn't an extra: it's part of how the city moves and how it is understood.

OUR IDEA

Use the ferry when the route improves

We prefer integrating it into a real day rather than turning it into an isolated activity without context.

Djurgården

One of the areas where the ferry fits naturally with museums and strolling.

Riddarholmen

A simple stop to enjoy views over the water without straying far from the center.

Waterside walks

Many great views appear simply by connecting areas on foot.

WHEN TO PRIORITIZE IT

If you love photography or slowing down

The water provides perspective and visual rest between visits. If your trip is very compact, take advantage of journeys you already need to make instead of adding an independent plan.
