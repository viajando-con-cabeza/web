◷DAYSHow many days do we recommend?+

For a first visit, we recommend 3 days. We feel this is the most balanced amount of time to enjoy the city without rushing.

♢SAFETYIs it safe to travel to Stockholm?+

In general, it is a comfortable city for travelers. As with any capital, it is wise to take standard precautions.

☼BEST TIME TO VISITWhen is the best time to go?+

From May to September, you will find more daylight hours and outdoor life. Winter offers a very different experience.

€BUDGETIs it an expensive city?+

Stockholm has high prices, especially for accommodation and dining, but with planning, it can be managed quite well.

🚇HOW TO GET AROUNDHow to get around Stockholm?+

The most practical combination is walking, the metro, and the ferry. Many of the main areas are very easy to explore on foot.

▱PAYMENTSCan I pay for everything by card?+

Yes. Sweden is very card-oriented, and on many trips, you will hardly need any cash.

⛴EXCURSIONSWhat to see near Stockholm?+

The archipelago is the perfect getaway to complement a city visit.

▤REQUIREMENTSDo I need a visa?+

Requirements depend on your nationality and are subject to change. Always check official information before your trip.
