The VCC SelectionTHE 16 PLACES, ONE BY ONE

What you'll find, how much time to set aside, and our advice for every stop.

01

GAMLA STAN

Getting lost is part of the journey

Gamla Stan is the best place to understand the origins of Stockholm. Its narrow, cobblestone streets wind through an old town filled with ochre facades, small squares, and churches. It is well worth wandering away from the main streets to discover quieter alleys.

Historic District · Stockholm · 2–3 hours · VCC Rating 5/5

VCC TIP

Walk through Stortorget and then wander off into the side streets; connect your stroll to Riddarholmen if you are looking for views and a bit more tranquility.

View on Google Maps
02

DJURGÅRDEN

The green side of Stockholm

Djurgården combines famous museums with green spaces and waterfront walks. Make sure to set aside time to explore the island itself, not just its museums.

Island · urban park · Stockholm · 2–4 hours · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

Don't try to hit all the museums in a single day. Choose the ones that truly interest you and save time for a walk along the water: strolling around the island is free.

View on Google Maps
03

CITY HALL

Red brick by the water

The City Hall and its red brick tower facing Lake Mälaren are a city icon. You can enjoy beautiful views of the historic center from the gardens.

Monument · Kungsholmen · 1–2 hours · VCC Rating 5/5

VCC TIP

If you don't want to pay for a guided tour, the exterior and the waterfront views alone are worth a stop. Check access to the interior spaces and the tower before you go.

View on Google Maps
04

SÖDERMALM

The city at a different pace

Södermalm offers small shops, cafes, residential streets, and viewpoints. Stroll through Mariatorget and head towards Monteliusvägen.

Neighborhood · Södermalm · 2–4 hours · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

A simple route is to start in Mariatorget, head towards Monteliusvägen, and continue discovering the neighborhood at your own pace. Wear comfortable shoes: you will encounter some hills.

View on Google Maps
05

MONTELIUSVÄGEN

Stockholm from above

This pedestrian walkway offers views of Lake Mälaren, Riddarholmen, Gamla Stan, and City Hall. It easily integrates into a visit to Södermalm.

Viewpoint · walk · Södermalm · 30–45 min · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

Incorporate this viewpoint into your Södermalm stroll instead of making a separate trip just to see it. If you are interested in the sunset light, check the sunset time and the forecast.

View on Google Maps
06

STORTORGET

The most iconic postcard view

Stortorget is the most famous square in Gamla Stan. Its colorful facades are a classic image of Stockholm and it is located near the Royal Palace and the cathedral.

Historic square · Gamla Stan · 20–30 min · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

Don't plan it as a standalone visit: it is located within Gamla Stan. Stop by to see the facades and then continue through the streets leading away from the square.

View on Google Maps
07

RIDDARHOLMEN

History, water, and pure tranquility

Very close to Gamla Stan, Riddarholmen features a medieval church, ancient palaces, and a walkway with views toward City Hall.

Historic island · City Center · 45–60 min · VCC Rating 3/5

VCC TIP

If you are already visiting Gamla Stan, cross over to Riddarholmen to see the City Hall from the shore. The outdoor walk is a quick stop and does not require an entrance fee.

View on Google Maps
08

STOCKHOLM METRO

An underground gallery

Many stations feature artworks, exposed rock, and stunning colors. T-Centralen, Stadion, and Kungsträdgården offer distinct styles.

Transport · Art · Various areas · 1–2 hours · VCC Rating 3/5

VCC TIP

To see part of the metro art without wasting half a day, combine T-Centralen, Stadion, and Kungsträdgården with your commutes. You will need a valid ticket.

View on Google Maps
09

FERRY RIDE

The city viewed from the water

A ferry allows you to understand the relationship between the islands and observe the old town from a different perspective. Some services are part of the public transport system.

Experience · Urban archipelago · 1–2 hours · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

Distinguish between the public ferry and the sightseeing cruise: they have different routes and prices. Check the pier and use it to connect two visits.

View on Google Maps
10

THE ROYAL PALACE

History in the heart of the city

The Royal Palace is next to Gamla Stan, and its surroundings integrate easily into a walk through the old town. You can visit its rooms according to your interests.

Palace · Gamla Stan · 1–2 hours · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

The exterior of the Royal Palace fits perfectly into a Gamla Stan walking route. If you aren't passionate about historical interiors, you might prefer spending that time strolling around; check tickets and opening hours beforehand.

View on Google Maps
11

STRÖMKAJEN

The city begins at its docks

A walk along the Strömkajen waterfront allows you to observe the city center from the water and connect different points of interest on foot.

Waterfront walk · City Center · 30–60 min · VCC Rating 3/5

VCC TIP

It is a great stretch for a walk by the water to watch the activity at the docks. If you want to board a boat, check which line departs from each pier beforehand.

View on Google Maps
12

VASA MUSEUM

A ship that changes everything

The Vasa preserves a nearly complete 17th-century warship that sank on its maiden voyage. Exploring its different levels allows you to see it from various heights and understand its history.

Museum · Djurgården · 2–3 hours · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

If you are only going to choose one museum, consider making the Vasa your priority. Plan for about two hours inside and combine it with a stroll through Djurgården; check opening hours and tickets before you head out.

View on Google Maps
13

SKANSEN

Traditions under the open sky

Skansen brings together traditional buildings and spaces dedicated to the cultural history of Sweden on the island of Djurgården.

Open-air museum · Djurgården · 2–4 hours · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

Book several hours if you decide to go inside. Check which buildings, demonstrations, and activities are operational on your date: the experience changes quite a bit depending on the season.

View on Google Maps
14

FOTOGRAFISKA

Stories in images

Fotografiska offers contemporary photography exhibitions by the waterfront in Södermalm. The programming changes throughout the year.

Museum · photography · Södermalm · 1–2 hours · VCC Rating 4/5

VCC TIP

Before buying your ticket, check which exhibitions are on during your trip. It is a visit that is especially worth it if you are interested in current photography programs.

View on Google Maps
15

KUNGSTRÄDGÅRDEN

A break in the heart of the city

This central park is a place to rest between sightseeing, stroll, and soak up the Stockholm atmosphere.

Urban park · Norrmalm · 30–60 min · VCC Rating 3/5

VCC TIP

Use it as a break between visits in Norrmalm, rather than a destination in itself. Its atmosphere changes with the seasons, so plan your visit according to the weather.

View on Google Maps
16

DROTTNINGHOLM PALACE

A royal escape among gardens

On the island of Lovön, on the outskirts of Stockholm, Drottningholm Palace combines Baroque architecture, historic gardens, and a UNESCO World Heritage site. It is an excursion independent of the city center tours; access to the interiors and other spaces depends on the season.

Palace and gardens · Lovön (Ekerö), outside the center · Half day including travel · No VCC rating published

VCC TIP

Book a half-day tour, transfers included. You can take a bus from Brommaplan or a boat from Stadshuskajen during the season. Confirm schedules and tickets.

View on Google Maps
