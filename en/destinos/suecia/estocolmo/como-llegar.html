Before booking your flight

STOCKHOLM AIRPORTS

Compare the airports serving Stockholm and the connections to reach your accommodation.

To make the right choice, check the distance, schedules, and the cost of getting to the city. A cheaper flight doesn't always mean a more economical transfer.

Stockholm Arlanda

ARN · The main airport for arriving in the capital and connecting to other cities in Sweden.

What we like

  • A great gateway for visiting Stockholm.
  • Several transfer alternatives to the city center.
  • Useful for continuing to other regions.

Things to keep in mind

  • Calculate the total transfer cost.
  • Compare schedules based on your arrival.
WEB

Stockholm Bromma

BMA · An airport close to Stockholm, with a more limited flight selection than Arlanda.

What we like

  • It is near the center of Stockholm.
  • It can simplify your transfer if your flight operates there.

Things to keep in mind

  • Check if there are flights for your dates.
  • Check the connection to your accommodation.
WEB

Stockholm Skavsta

NYO · Airport located in Nyköping, quite a bit further from the center of Stockholm.

What we like

  • An alternative if you find a suitable flight.
  • It can work for routes through the south.
  • Allows you to compare other arrival options.

Things to keep in mind

  • The transfer to Stockholm takes longer.
  • A cheap flight isn't always the best deal.
WEB

Small changes, big difference

HOW TO SAVE

Seven tips to pay less for transfers to Stockholm.

Compare flight + transfer

A cheap flight to Skavsta may no longer be cheap once you add the round-trip bus. Compare the total cost to your accommodation, not just the plane ticket.

Look for online rates

Check the operator's website or app before paying at the airport. Flygbussarna offers online prices, and Arlanda Express publishes advance and group rates.

Don't forget Arlanda C

The SL commuter train that passes through Arlanda Central requires an access supplement for adults. Compare it with the bus and Arlanda Express before deciding.

Rate the bus

If you're not in a hurry, compare the direct bus with the express train. The minutes you save might not be worth the price difference, especially if you're traveling as a couple.

Check round-trip options

Before buying two one-way tickets, compare round-trip fares and discounts based on age or group travel. Also, check if your ticket allows changes.

Avoid unexpected taxi costs

If you land late, check the last public transport departure and how much time you'll need to collect your luggage. This will help you avoid an expensive last-minute alternative.

Save with a car

If you arrive by car, compare the cost of parking in the city center for several days against a peripheral parking lot and public transport to your hotel.
