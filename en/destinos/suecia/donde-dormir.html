HOTELS

A great base for visiting cities

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • In Stockholm, Gothenburg, and Malmö, you will find chains, boutique, and design hotels.
  • Options abound near train stations and public transport.
  • There are also small hotels and inns in towns and rural areas.
  • In tourist areas, supply and prices vary significantly depending on the season.

PLEASE NOTE

  • Prioritize the real-time travel time, not just the distance to the center.
  • Check if breakfast is included and its hours.
  • Review room size, bed type, and cancellation policies.
  • If you are traveling by car, check for parking availability and potential extra fees.
VCC TIP

In Stockholm, we found that having good connections was key to making the most of the day.

APARTMENTS

More freedom for families and long stays

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • In cities, there are studios and full apartments; also in holiday areas.
  • The kitchen allows you to organize meals and schedules over several nights.
  • There is accommodation available for couples, families, and groups with more common space.
  • Amenities vary: from simple kitchens to washing machines and terraces.

PLEASE NOTE

  • Add cleaning, fees, and deposits at the price shown.
  • Check if it includes sheets, towels and kitchen utensils.
  • Read the check-in and key return instructions.
  • If you don't have a car, check the connection to public transport and supermarkets.
VCC TIP

We would compare the final cost and the savings on meals before choosing an apartment.

CABINS · STUGOR

A peaceful stay among forests and lakes

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • The stugor they are very common near lakes, forests, and campsites.
  • They range from simple cabins to full houses with kitchens and heating.
  • You will also find them on islands and in archipelago areas.
  • In rural destinations, tranquility is part of the experience.

PLEASE NOTE

  • Check if sheets, towels, and final cleaning are charged separately.
  • Check for bathrooms, hot water, heating, and actual amenities.
  • On islands, check ferry schedules; inland, check for car-free access.
  • In winter, confirm opening times and snow access.
VCC TIP

After visiting the archipelago, we would choose a stuga to enjoy it at a slower pace.

HOSTELS AND LODGES

Save money without sacrificing a good location

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Hostels are found in cities, islands, and nature areas. They aren't just for young people: they also welcome families and hikers.
  • They usually combine shared dorms and private rooms.
  • Some have communal kitchens; the
  • STF network offers options throughout the country.

PLEASE NOTE

  • The sheets and towels may be for rent or not included.
  • Check whether you are paying per bed or for an entire room.
  • Check bathrooms, lockers, reception hours, and quiet hours.
  • In remote areas, check transportation and nearby services.
VCC TIP

If we were looking to save money, we would look at location, rest, and bedding first.

CAMPSITES

Nature and freedom for a road trip

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Sweden has campsites by lakes, coasts, and forests throughout the country.
  • You can find pitches and cabins within the same premises.
  • The most well-equipped ones feature showers, a kitchen, water, and electricity.
  • In natural areas, there are more basic and tranquil options.

PLEASE NOTE

  • Confirm opening season and available services.
  • Check surcharges for electricity, showers, and pets.
  • In summer, book in advance for popular coasts and islands.
  • Wild camping has its own rules: you are not allowed to park vehicles anywhere.
VCC TIP

At a Swedish campsite, we would first check the season and the services.

GLAMPING

A different kind of night with more comfort

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • There are fully equipped shops and special stays in forests and archipelagos.
  • Some accommodations include a bed and heating; others are very basic.
  • The experience is usually linked to the landscape and disconnecting from it all.
  • In certain locations, the accommodation is far from urban centers.

PLEASE NOTE

  • Check if bathroom, shower, water, and electricity are private.
  • It may require walking, driving, or taking a boat to reach.
  • Verify if meals, equipment, and activities are included.
  • Check the weather, opening season, and cancellation policies.
VCC TIP

We would choose it for the surroundings, making sure to check exactly what is included.

UNIQUE ACCOMMODATIONS

When sleeping is also part of the journey

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • Sweden offers ice hotels, treehouses, and floating cabins.
  • In Lapland, there is no shortage of winter and landscape-focused experiences.
  • There are also design-led options nestled by lakes and forests in the south.
  • Some combine accommodation with saunas, gastronomy, or activities.

PLEASE NOTE

  • Check season and weather conditions before booking.
  • Access may require a car or an organized transfer.
  • Check the room type and which experiences are included in the rate.
  • Consider the total value: it is an experience, not always the baseline for the entire trip.
VCC TIP

For us, a unique stay must justify its price through the experience.

HOW TO SAVE

Seven tips to save money without sacrificing quality accommodation.

BOOK IN ADVANCE

Compare prices in advance for popular summer areas and for Lapland during the snow season.

COMPARE REGIONS

Staying in Stockholm or Gothenburg doesn't have the same cost or travel times as staying in the countryside.

VIEW CONNECTION

If you aren't driving, check train and bus schedules. In rural areas, the last connection of the day is especially important.

FLEXIBLE DATES

Changing your dates can reduce the cost of cabins, hotels, or apartments, especially during peak season.

FLEXIBLE CANCELLATION

If you are planning a multi-region route, check the deadlines for modifying or canceling overnight stays.

CHECK THE FINAL PRICE

Check for taxes, cleaning fees, linens, breakfast, parking, and accommodation capacity.

CHOOSE WHAT YOU NEED

For cities, prioritize connections; for nature, compare cabins, apartments, and campsites based on your vehicle.
