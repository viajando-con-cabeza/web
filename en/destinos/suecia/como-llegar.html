AIRPORTS IN SWEDEN

Choose the most convenient airport based on the first stop of your itinerary.

The cheapest airport isn't always the most convenient: before choosing, consider the distance to your first stop, the cost of transfers, and the connections to the rest of your route. These options will help you compare where to start your trip to Sweden.

Stockholm Arlanda

ARN · The main airport for arriving in the capital and connecting to other cities in Sweden.

What we like

  • A great gateway for visiting Stockholm.
  • Several transfer alternatives to the city center.
  • Useful for continuing to other regions.

Things to keep in mind

  • Calculate the total transfer cost.
  • Compare schedules based on your arrival.
WEB

Gothenburg Landvetter

GOT · A useful airport if your itinerary begins in Gothenburg or along the west coast.

What we like

  • Allows you to start your route from the west.
  • Practical for visiting Gothenburg.
  • Avoid arriving in Stockholm first.

Things to keep in mind

  • Add the journey to your accommodation.
  • Check connections to your destination.
WEB

Stockholm Skavsta

NYO · Airport located in Nyköping, quite a bit further from the center of Stockholm.

What we like

  • An alternative if you find a suitable flight.
  • It can work for routes through the south.
  • Allows you to compare other arrival options.

Things to keep in mind

  • The transfer to Stockholm takes longer.
  • A cheap flight isn't always the best deal.
WEB

Malmö

MMX · An entry option to southern Sweden if you start in Malmö or the Skåne region.

What we like

  • Good location for exploring the south.
  • Allows you to avoid passing through Stockholm.
  • Useful for multi-stop routes.

Things to keep in mind

  • Compare flights to Copenhagen as well.
  • Keep the transfer to Malmö in mind.
WEB

Luleå

LLA · Northern airport to start an itinerary along the Baltic coast and its surroundings.

What we like

  • Allows you to start directly in the north.
  • Useful for routes near Luleå.
  • An alternative to traveling across the entire country by land.

Things to keep in mind

  • Distances between destinations can be long.
  • Check the connections from the airport.
WEB

Kiruna

KRN · Arrival point for traveling through Swedish Lapland and its extreme northern landscapes.

What we like

  • Provides access to the interior of Lapland.
  • Practical for starting a route through the north.
  • Avoids a very long transfer from the south.

Things to keep in mind

  • Organize local transport in advance.
  • Weather conditions may affect travel plans.

HOW TO SAVE MONEY WHEN ARRIVING IN SWEDEN

Seven tips to save on flights, transfers, and connections depending on your route.

Compare flight + transfer

Don't just look at the airfare. Add up baggage, transport to your accommodation, and the commute to the start of your route.

Consider Copenhagen

If you are heading to Malmö or Skåne, compare Copenhagen with Swedish airports and include the cost of the train to Sweden.

Avoid tight connections

Leave a buffer between flights, trains, and ferries, especially if you have purchased separate tickets.

Compare train and bus

You can save money on some airport transfers by taking the bus; check the duration and the last departure time.

Think about a linear route

Entering through one airport and leaving from another can prevent backtracking. Compare the total multi-city price.

Check real-time schedules

Check how you will get to your accommodation if you land late or if transport runs less frequently.

Don't rent a car out of habit

If you start in major cities, it might be more worthwhile to travel by train and pick up a car later on.
