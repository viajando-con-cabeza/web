A NORMAL COUPLE.
A HUGE PASSION FOR TRAVEL.

We don't live on the road, nor do we have an infinite budget. We are a working couple that saves up and seizes every opportunity to discover new places.

Over time, planning trips has become more than just choosing a destination: it's about finding the best way to do it—spending less, making better use of time, and enjoying every moment to the fullest.
