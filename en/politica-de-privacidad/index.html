INFORMATION AND TRANSPARENCY

PRIVACY POLICY

We want you to enjoy Viajando con Cabeza with the peace of mind that comes from knowing how we handle your data.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Who is the controller?

The controller responsible for the data collected through this website is:

  • Controller: Viajando con Cabeza
  • Website: viajandoconcabeza.com
  • Email: info@viajandoconcabeza.com

Before publishing this page permanently, complete the identification details of the owner (name or business name and address) and, if applicable, Tax ID. The blog name alone may not be sufficient to legally identify the controller.

2. What data can we collect?

Depending on how you use the website, we may process:

  • Data that you voluntarily provide in the contact form, such as name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Basic technical data that the server may record when visiting the website, such as IP address, date and time of access, browser, or requested pages, to ensure its operation and security.
  • Data related to cookie preferences, if the website incorporates cookies or similar technologies that require them.

3. What do we use your data for and what is the legal basis?

  • To respond to your inquiries or proposals: we use the data you include in the form to address the message and maintain communication. The legal basis is your request and, where applicable, your consent when submitting the form.
  • To manage and protect the website: we may process technical data to maintain the site, prevent abusive use, and resolve incidents. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in the security and correct functioning of the site.
  • Legal obligations: we may process or retain information when necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

We will not use the data sent via the form to send you advertising or newsletters without first requesting your corresponding consent.

4. How long do we keep it?

Messages and contact data will be kept for the time necessary to respond to and manage the inquiry. Afterward, they will be deleted or blocked for the periods that may be required for legal liabilities. Technical logs will be kept for the period necessary for security and maintenance, according to the hosting configuration.

5. Who may receive your data?

To provide and maintain the website, we may use providers acting as data processors, such as web hosting, email services, or form management tools. Specifically, the contact form is processed via Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare's email service, and messages are sent to our management inbox (Gmail) associated with info@viajandoconcabeza.comThese providers will process the data in accordance with their terms and applicable instructions.

We do not sell your personal data. It will only be communicated to third parties when necessary to provide the service, when there is a legal obligation, or if you have given your consent.

6. Are international transfers carried out?

Some technology providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. If this occurs, the safeguards provided by data protection regulations will apply, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses, where applicable. Please also consult the privacy policies of the providers you use.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use technical cookies necessary for its operation. If we incorporate analytical, advertising, affiliate, or third-party cookies that are not strictly necessary, they will be disclosed and consent will be requested before they are activated, as required by regulations. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, if available, through the website's preference panel.

Links to third-party websites—for example, booking platforms, social networks, or affiliate services—are governed by the privacy policies of those third parties.

8. Your rights

You may request access to your personal data, as well as its rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing, and, where applicable, portability or the withdrawal of consent. To exercise these rights, write to info@viajandoconcabeza.com, indicating which request you wish to make. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

You may also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) if you believe the processing does not comply with regulations: www.aepd.es.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. No system connected to the internet can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, the services used, or regulations. We will publish the current version on this page and indicate the date of the update.
