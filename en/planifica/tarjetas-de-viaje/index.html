Small tips, big savings

MISTAKES TO AVOID

What to check before paying or withdrawing cash abroad.

Always pay in the local currency

If the terminal offers to charge you in euros, choose the local currency. You will avoid the merchant's conversion rate, which is usually worse.

Prioritize card payments over ATMs

Whenever possible, pay by card at shops. Withdrawing cash can add fees from both your bank and the ATM itself.

Use debit for daily expenses

If you have a balance and good exchange rates, debit helps you control your spending without financing purchases or generating interest.

Check security deposits before you travel

To rent a car or for certain hotel deposits, credit cards may be required. Confirm the type of card accepted before booking.

Don't forget limits and surcharges

Check currency exchange fees, monthly fees, withdrawal limits, and after-hours or weekend surcharges: these depend on your plan and bank.

Carry a backup card

Keep a separate alternative in case your primary card fails, is lost, or gets blocked. Enable notifications to detect unauthorized charges.
