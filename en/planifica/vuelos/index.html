    HOW TO SAVE

    Eight ideas to avoid unnecessary expenses when booking flights.

    Compare the total price

    Includes luggage, seat selection, transfers, and meals. The cheapest option isn't always the most economical.

    Be flexible with your dates

    Check nearby dates and different times before deciding.

    Check your baggage carefully

    Verify dimensions, weight, and whether the fare includes everything you need.

    Check the airport

    Calculate the transport cost to your accommodation: distant airports cost extra too.

    Seat selection is optional

    Decide if it's worth paying to choose your seat, especially on short flights.

    Plan your meals

    Eating beforehand, bringing permitted snacks, or buying on board changes the total cost.

    Monitor your layovers

    Check terminals, duration, and ensure you have enough time between connections.

    Read the terms and conditions

    Before paying, check changes, cancellations, and what is included in the fare.
