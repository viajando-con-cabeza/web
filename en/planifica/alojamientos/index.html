Booking platforms

OUR EXPERIENCE

The platforms we use, their advantages, and what to check before booking.

Booking.com

Wide selection of hotels, apartments, and accommodations for almost any destination.

What we like

  • Great variety to compare your options.
  • Useful filters to find exactly what you're looking for.
  • Traveler reviews to help you decide.

Things to keep in mind

  • The price isn't always the lowest.
  • Some rates come with stricter cancellation policies.
WEB

Airbnb

Apartments and houses to stay in with more space or for a different kind of experience.

What we like

  • Options with kitchens and other amenities.
  • Great for families and groups.
  • Accommodations with their own unique character.

Things to keep in mind

  • Check cleaning and service fees.
  • Terms and conditions depend on each host.
WEB

Grab it

An alternative to consider for hotels, getaways, and special deals.

What we like

  • Promotions in certain destinations and dates.
  • Allows you to compare certain getaways and packages.
  • Simple search process.

Things to keep in mind

  • You won't always find as much variety.
  • Offers change based on date and availability.

Tips for better booking

HOW TO SAVE

Six tips to pay less and book with peace of mind.

Compare the final amount

Don't just look at the nightly rate: add taxes, cleaning fees, services, and any mandatory charges.

Try nearby dates

Changing your check-in or check-out by one or two days can unlock more affordable options.

Check the hotel's website

Look for the same accommodation on its official website and compare the rate and conditions.

Evaluate the cancellation policy

A flexible rate can be worth it if your itinerary might still change; check the cancellation deadline.

Double-check the location

A cheaper hotel far from the center can end up costing more due to travel expenses and time.

Take advantage of real discounts

Compare promotions and loyalty benefits, but only if they actually improve the final price.
