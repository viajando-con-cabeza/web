HOW TO COMPARE INSURANCE

Choose the coverage that fits your trip, not just the lowest price.

Compare actual coverage

Don't just compare prices: check medical assistance, repatriation, luggage, and cancellation, along with their limits.

Review exclusions and activities

Confirm whether your travel activities, altitude, rescue services, and medical conditions are covered.

Evaluate duration and destination

Choose a policy valid for all your days and countries. Medical limits may vary depending on the destination.

Check deductibles and advances

Find out how much you would have to pay out of pocket and if the insurer provides upfront payments in an emergency.

Check 24-hour assistance

Before traveling, save the assistance number, check available languages, and the procedure for filing a claim.

The essentials

MUST-HAVE COVERAGE

The guarantees you must check before booking.

Medical assistance

Emergencies, hospitalization, tests, and medical expense limits.

Repatriation

Medical transfer or return when conditions allow.

Cancellation

Non-recoverable expenses for expressly covered causes.

Baggage

Theft, loss, or damage: check limits and required documentation.

Activities

Coverage for sports, excursions, and rescues where applicable.

Personal liability

Unintentional damage to third parties if coverage is included.

24-hour assistance

Contact details, language, and steps to follow in an emergency.

Watch out for this

MISTAKES TO AVOID

Avoid being left without coverage when you need it most.

Choosing based on price alone

A cheap insurance policy may have insufficient limits or significant exclusions.

Not reading the exclusions

Certain activities, illnesses, or situations may not be covered by the policy.

Booking too late

Cancellation coverage may require specific timeframes and conditions from the moment of booking.

Assuming cancellation is always covered

Only expressly included causes are eligible for reimbursement, up to the specified limits.

Failing to review medical expenses

Check maximum coverage amounts and who is responsible for advancing hospital payments.

Not keeping your documents

Keep your policy, emergency contacts, receipts, and supporting documents safe.
