💡 VCC Tip

For Bali, we would pack light, breathable clothing and a long piece of fabric for temples. Repellent and sun protection are top priorities.

⊘ Not necessary to bring

  • Heavy clothing
  • Too many shoes
  • Large towels if provided by your accommodation
  • Plenty of cash on hand

🛒 You can buy things there

  • Sarong if needed
  • Water and daily essentials
  • Simple rain poncho
  • Toiletries

What to pack based on your type of trip

Beach

Swimsuit, sunscreen, and a lightweight bag.

Motorbike

Closed-toe shoes and something to cover up from the sun.

Waterfalls and nature

Shoes with good grip and a waterproof bag.

Temples

Shoulders and legs covered where appropriate.
