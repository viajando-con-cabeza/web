💡 VCC Tip

The wind significantly changes the wind chill factor. Even if it's sunny, we would always bring a light layer or a windbreaker.

⊘ Not necessary to bring

  • Too many formal clothes
  • Heavy suitcase
  • Several similar pairs of shoes
  • Thick coat only if absolutely necessary

🛒 You can buy things there

  • Water
  • Sun protection
  • Trail snacks
  • Beach Essentials

What to pack based on your type of trip

Beaches

Swimsuit, light towel, and sunscreen.

Road trip

Comfortable clothes and a small backpack.

Hiking Trails

Shoes with good grip.

Sunsets

A light layer for the wind.
