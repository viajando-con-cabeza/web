💡 VCC Tip

We would bring light clothing, but also a light layer for shopping malls and places with strong air conditioning.

⊘ Not necessary to bring

  • Heavy coat unless for a specific excursion
  • Too many evening outfits
  • Lots of cash
  • Uncomfortable footwear

🛒 You can buy things there

  • Water
  • Sun protection
  • Toiletries
  • Basic clothing in case you forget something

What to pack based on your type of trip

City

Light and comfortable clothing.

Desert

Closed-toe shoes, sunscreen, and light layers.

Beach

Swimsuit, flip-flops, and sunscreen.

Dinner or rooftop bar

Something a bit more dressy if you have plans.
