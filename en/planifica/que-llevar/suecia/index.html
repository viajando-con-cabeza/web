💡 VCC Tip

Prioritize layering and a good waterproof jacket over filling your suitcase with very thick clothing.

⊘ Not necessary to bring

  • Too effective
  • Four pairs of shoes
  • Very elegant clothes, except for a specific plan
  • Too many heavy items instead of layers

🛒 You can buy things there

  • Toiletries
  • Basic medications
  • Warm clothing if you're missing anything
  • Simple winter accessories

What to pack based on your type of trip

City break in Stockholm

Comfortable and slightly more urban clothing.

Nature and hiking

Technical layers and high-traction footwear.

Road trip through Sweden

More leeway for weather changes.

Snow or northern lights

Maximum protection against the cold.
