💡 VCC Tip

For Paris, we would recommend versatile clothing and very good footwear. It is better to re-wear clothes than to carry a huge suitcase.

⊘ Not necessary to bring

  • Huge suitcase
  • Uncomfortable footwear
  • Lots of cash
  • Too many different outfits

🛒 You can buy things there

  • Umbrella if needed
  • Pharmacy essentials
  • Toiletries
  • Small accessories by weather

What to pack based on your type of trip

City break

Few items, easy to mix and match.

Museums

Light layers and a small backpack.

Rain

Compact umbrella and suitable footwear.

Disneyland

Comfort, battery pack, and a layer for changing weather.
