💡 VCC Tip

On a road trip, we would prioritize comfort, light packing, and layering. The less you have to move at each hotel, the better.

⊘ Not necessary to bring

  • Huge suitcase for a road trip
  • Too much formal clothing
  • Items you can easily buy
  • Lots of cash

🛒 You can buy things there

  • Water and road snacks
  • Pharmacy essentials
  • A few items depending on the local weather
  • Simple car accessories

What to pack based on your type of trip

Road trip

Luggage that is easy to load and unload.

National parks

Proper footwear, water, and layers.

Major cities

Comfortable sneakers and a light backpack.

Coast

Light clothing and something for the wind.
