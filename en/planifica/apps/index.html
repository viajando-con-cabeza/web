MISTAKES TO AVOID

Seven common mistakes when booking excursions and tickets.

Booking without checking the language

A tour may have several versions and schedules: check which language it is conducted in before paying.

Not comparing cancellation policies

Two similar tours can have very different rules. Check the cancellation deadline and any potential fees.

Confusing a free tour with a no-cost activity

A tip is usually expected on these tours; keep this in mind when planning your budget.

Arriving late to the meeting point

Confirm the exact location, how to get there, and if you need to arrive before the departure time.

Paying twice for the same ticket

Check if your city pass or combo tour already includes the museum or activity.

Not checking what the booking includes

Transport, tickets, meals, and guides may be included or may be extra, depending on the operator.

Always relying on an internet connection

Download your tickets, confirmation, address, and a map before you head out.
